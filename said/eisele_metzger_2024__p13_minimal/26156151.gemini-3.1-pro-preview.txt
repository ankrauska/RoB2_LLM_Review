Based on the provided manuscript, protocol, and bias criteria, here is the step-by-step risk of bias assessment for the endpoint **occurrence of cardiac death**:

**1. Risk of bias arising from the randomization process: low risk**
*Justification:* The study used an "interactive web-based response system" for randomization, which typically ensures proper random sequence generation and robust allocation concealment. Baseline characteristics were well balanced between the IVUS-guided and angiography-guided groups, suggesting that the randomization process was successful. 

**2. Risk of bias due to deviations from the intended interventions: low risk**
*Justification:* Although physicians and participants could not be blinded to the intervention (IVUS vs. conventional angiography), deviations from intended interventions are unlikely to systematically affect the hard objective outcome of cardiac death. Furthermore, the analysis was explicitly stated to have been performed according to the intention-to-treat principle.

**3. Risk of bias due to missing outcome data: low risk**
*Justification:* The follow-up completion rate was extremely high (99.5% in the IVUS-guided group and 100% in the angiography-guided group). Missing outcome data was negligible and therefore unlikely to introduce any clinically meaningful bias.

**4. Risk of bias in measurement of the outcome: low risk**
*Justification:* Cardiac death is a hard, highly objective endpoint that is not susceptible to detection bias due to a lack of patient blinding. Furthermore, the study explicitly states that "All clinical events were independently monitored and assessed by a clinical event committee blinded to randomization," ensuring objective adjudication of the outcome. 

**5. Risk of bias in selection of the reported result: low risk**
*Justification:* The protocol (NCT01563952) pre-specified the primary endpoint as the "event rate of cardiac death within 12 months." The manuscript reports exactly this endpoint without selective reporting or alterations to the timeframes or definitions.

**6. Overall rating: low risk**
*Justification:* The risk of bias is judged as "low risk" for all five assessed domains. Therefore, the overall risk of bias for the outcome of cardiac death is low.
