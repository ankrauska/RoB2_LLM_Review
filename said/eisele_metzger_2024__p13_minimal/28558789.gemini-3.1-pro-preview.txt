Based on the provided manuscript and protocol, here is the risk of bias assessment for the **Motor Assessment Scale item 6 (Upper Arm Function, MAS6)** endpoint:

**1. Risk of bias arising from the randomization process: low-risk**
(Justification: The study used a computer-generated random number sequence (1:1) for sequence generation. Allocation concealment was ensured using consecutively numbered opaque envelopes collected by an independent offsite investigator. There were no baseline imbalances of concern that would suggest a failure of randomization, especially considering the small sample size (n=4).)

**2. Risk of bias due to deviations from the intended interventions: low-risk**
(Justification: The trial was described as triple-blind. The study personnel involved in assessment and training, along with all participants, were blinded to group allocation. Active and sham tRNS were delivered using identical setups. There were no reported deviations from the intended intervention, and all participants completed the 12 training sessions.)

**3. Risk of bias due to missing outcome data: low-risk**
(Justification: Outcome data for the MAS6 were available for all four randomized participants at all time points (baseline, post-training, and follow-up). There was no missing data or attrition.)

**4. Risk of bias in measurement of the outcome: low-risk**
(Justification: The MAS6 is a standardized clinical assessment tool. Assessors were blinded to the intervention allocation, and participants were also blinded, which minimizes the risk of assessor or performance bias influencing the outcome measurement.)

**5. Risk of bias in selection of the reported result: some concerns**
(Justification: While the study was prospectively registered, the registry protocol specified the primary outcome as a "Composite score of Motor Assessment Scale upper limb items 6-8". However, the manuscript reports MAS6 as the primary outcome and MAS7 and MAS8 as secondary outcomes separately. This discrepancy between the registered protocol and the published report raises some concerns regarding the selective reporting of outcome measures.)

**6. Overall rating: some concerns**
(Justification: The overall risk of bias is judged to be of 'some concerns' primarily due to the discrepancy between the prospectively registered primary outcome (a composite score) and the published primary outcome (individual MAS6 score), which falls under the domain of risk of bias in the selection of the reported result. All other domains were rated as low risk.)
