Based on the provided manuscript, here is the assessment of the risk of bias for the endpoint **failure rate**:

**1. Risk of bias arising from the randomization process: low-risk**
(Justification: The study utilized a block randomization method to assign the interventions in a split-mouth cross-quadrant design. The assignment of the upper right quadrant was randomized, which intrinsically randomized the other quadrants. Allocation concealment was adequate as the operators were unaware of the assignment until the patient was entered into the trial. There is no evidence of baseline imbalances that would suggest a problem with the randomization process.)

**2. Risk of bias due to deviations from the intended interventions: low-risk**
(Justification: Although the operators were not blinded due to the different application methods of the two etching systems, the patients were kept unaware of the assignment. Given the objective nature of bracket bonding and the split-mouth design where both interventions are applied within the same patient, it is highly unlikely that the lack of operator blinding led to systematic deviations from the intended interventions that would meaningfully affect the outcome.)

**3. Risk of bias due to missing outcome data: low-risk**
(Justification: The follow-up was essentially complete. Out of 39 patients, only one patient moved away after 7 months (with no bracket failures up to that point) and their data was appropriately included in the survival analysis. The missingness is extremely minimal and not related to the true outcome, thus posing no risk of bias.)

**4. Risk of bias in measurement of the outcome: low-risk**
(Justification: The outcome (bracket failure rate) is an objective clinical event. While operators evaluating the outcome may not have been blinded, it is highly unlikely that knowledge of the intervention influenced the assessment of whether a bracket remained bonded to a tooth or had failed, as this is a straightforward, binary objective measure.)

**5. Risk of bias in selection of the reported result: low-risk**
(Justification: Although a pre-registered trial protocol is not explicitly referenced, the reported outcomes correspond entirely to the stated objectives in the methodology. Standard descriptive and survival analyses (Kaplan-Meier, Cox regression) were performed appropriately for a 12-month follow-up period, and there is no indication of selective reporting of multiple measurements or analysis methods.)

**6. Overall rating: low-risk**
(Justification: All domains were assessed as having a low risk of bias, leading to an overall judgement of low risk of bias for this outcome.)
