1. Risk of bias arising from the randomization process: low-risk (Patients were randomly assigned using a computer-generated list maintained by a third party not involved in the study's conduction, ensuring allocation concealment. Baseline characteristics were well balanced between the groups, and there is no indication that the randomization process was flawed.)

2. Risk of bias due to deviations from the intended interventions: low-risk (The trial was double-blinded. Placebo and treatment tablets were identical in appearance, packaging, labeling, and taste. The analysis was explicitly stated to be strictly intention-to-treat, irrespective of patient adherence to the study medication.)

3. Risk of bias due to missing outcome data: low-risk (Although a few patients discontinued the study medication due to adverse effects (4 in the treatment group and 3 in the placebo group), they were followed up and assessed according to the protocol. There were some deaths during the 12-month follow-up (2 in the treatment group and 5 in the placebo group), but the overall amount of missing data is small, balanced, and unlikely to have introduced substantial bias in the measurement of the outcome.)

4. Risk of bias in measurement of the outcome: low-risk (The subjective global assessment (SGA) score is a subjective outcome. However, the potential for bias was minimized because both the patients and the investigators/assessors were blinded to the intervention. Specifically, the SGA was performed by two trained observers who were blinded to treatment group allocation and biochemical results, and their inter-rater reliability was confirmed to be excellent prior to the study.)

5. Risk of bias in selection of the reported result: low-risk (The SGA score was pre-specified as the primary outcome measure. The data were reported for all pre-planned time points using appropriate statistical methods (repeated measures ANOVA) as described in the methodology. There is no evidence of selective reporting or data dredging.)

6. Overall rating: low risk (All assessed domains are judged to be at a low risk of bias, leading to high confidence in the reported results for this endpoint.)
