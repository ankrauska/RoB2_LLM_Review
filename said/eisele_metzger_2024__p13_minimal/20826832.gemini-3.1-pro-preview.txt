Based on the provided manuscript, here is the assessment of the risk of bias for the endpoint **compliance with mammogram screening**:

**1. Risk of bias arising from the randomization process: low-risk**
(Justification: The study used a computerized random number generator for sequence generation. Allocation was concealed, as baseline data was collected and sent to an external coordinating center (Fred Hutchinson Cancer Research Center) to proceed with random allocation using stratified block randomization. Baseline characteristics were well-balanced across the three groups, indicating a successful and concealed randomization process.)

**2. Risk of bias due to deviations from the intended interventions: low-risk**
(Justification: Although participants and interventionists could not be blinded, which is standard for this type of behavioral intervention, the outcome was measured using an intention-to-treat (ITT) framework. The analysis appropriately included all randomized participants according to their assigned groups, and there is no indication that deviations from the intended interventions affected the objective outcome.)

**3. Risk of bias due to missing outcome data: low-risk**
(Justification: The primary outcome of mammogram screening compliance was assessed using electronic records. The authors state that an intention-to-treat analysis was conducted where electronic clinical records "were reviewed for all women and compliance was estimated using the baseline denominators of each group." Therefore, there was no missing data for the objective primary outcome.)

**4. Risk of bias in measurement of the outcome: low-risk**
(Justification: The primary endpoint of mammography compliance was measured objectively using electronic health and radiology records, reducing the risk of self-report bias. Furthermore, the manuscript states that "Staff at the clinic and at the radiology unit where participants got their mammograms were blinded to the allocation group," meaning the outcome assessors were unaware of the intervention assignments.)

**5. Risk of bias in selection of the reported result: low-risk**
(Justification: The reported results align with the pre-specified methodology outlined in the methods section. The main outcome (mammogram screening rates) was analyzed and reported for all randomized groups without evidence of selective reporting or multiple unadjusted statistical comparisons.)

**6. Overall rating: low risk**
(Justification: All assessed domains are rated as low risk of bias. The trial was well-designed, utilized robust external randomization, employed objective blinded outcome measurements, and properly analyzed the data using an intention-to-treat approach.)
