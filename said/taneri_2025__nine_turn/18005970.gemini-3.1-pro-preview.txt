**Overall Risk-of-bias judgement:** High 

**Reasoning:** 
Following the Cochrane RoB 2 guidelines, a study is judged to be at an overall "High risk of bias" if it scores "High" in at least one domain, *or* if it scores "Some concerns" across multiple domains in a way that substantially lowers confidence in the result. 

In this evaluation, the study accumulated "Some concerns" across three critical domains:
1. **Domain 1 (Randomization process):** There was a complete lack of information regarding allocation concealment.
2. **Domain 2 (Deviations from intended interventions):** Participants and personnel were completely unblinded, with no information provided to rule out context-driven deviations.
3. **Domain 5 (Selection of the reported result):** There was no pre-specified trial protocol or statistical analysis plan available. Furthermore, the authors made a data-driven analytical choice to dichotomize a continuous outcome (number of errors) using the sample's median as a cut-off (a threshold determined *after* the data was collected), which introduces a high risk of analytical flexibility and selective reporting.

As you requested a robust and critical evaluation, the compounding effect of these three domains—particularly the lack of blinding combined with post-hoc, data-driven analytical choices without a registered protocol—substantially lowers confidence in the estimated effect. Therefore, the overall risk of bias is elevated to **High**.
