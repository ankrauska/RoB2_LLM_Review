Outcome Name: survival time

**Domain 3: Missing outcome data**

1. 3.1 Were data for this outcome available for all, or nearly all, participants randomized?
   * Response: Probably no
   * Reason: Out of 13 randomized patients, 1 patient in the placebo group (allocated n=6) dropped out and was excluded from the survival analysis. While a single dropout might normally be considered negligible, in a remarkably small trial like this one, losing 1 out of 6 patients (16.7%) in the control arm means the missing data could potentially have an important impact on the estimated hazard ratio for survival.

2. 3.2 Is there evidence that the result was not biased by missing outcome data?
   * Response: No
   * Reason: The authors simply state that the single dropped-out patient was excluded so that 12 patients were analyzed. There is no mention of sensitivity analyses or advanced imputation techniques to prove the result was not biased by this exclusion.

3. 3.3 Could missingness in the outcome depend on its true value?
   * Response: Probably yes
   * Reason: Sporadic Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease (sCJD) is a rapidly progressive and fatal disease. Loss to follow-up or study dropout in such a context could very well be related to the patient's rapidly deteriorating health status or death (which is the actual outcome being measured). 

4. 3.4 Is it likely that missingness in the outcome depended on its true value?
   * Response: Probably no
   * Reason: Although the missingness *could* depend on the true value, there is only a single dropout in the entire trial, and the authors do not provide the specific reason for this dropout. Without differing dropout rates between groups or explicitly reported reasons linking the dropout to the outcome, there is insufficient evidence to conclude that it is *likely* (which would indicate a high risk of bias).

**Risk-of-bias judgement for Domain 3:** Some concerns
