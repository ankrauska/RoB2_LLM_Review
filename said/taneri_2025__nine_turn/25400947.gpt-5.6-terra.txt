### 1.3. Did baseline differences between intervention groups suggest a problem with the randomization process?

- **Response:** Yes  
- **Reason:** Although group sizes were equal (300 participants per group), the article reports a statistically significant baseline difference in cervical effacement: mean effacement was 35.93% (SD 18.07) in the MgSO4 group and 42.0% (SD 15.64) in the celecoxib group (*P* ≤ 0.0001). This pronounced imbalance suggests a potential problem with the randomization process.

### Risk-of-bias judgement for Domain 1: **High risk of bias**

- **Reason:** The method used to generate the random allocation sequence and the method used to conceal allocation are not reported. In addition, there was a marked and statistically significant imbalance in baseline cervical effacement, a clinically relevant prognostic characteristic for delivery after 48 hours.
