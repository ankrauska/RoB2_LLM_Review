Outcome Name: subjective global assessment (SGA) score

**Domain 3: Risk of bias due to missing outcome data**

1. 3.1 Were data for this outcome available for all, or nearly all, participants randomized?
   * Response: Probably no
   * Reason: Although the 7 patients who discontinued the study medication were followed up for nutritional assessment, there were 7 deaths, 2 transfers to long-term hemodialysis, and 1 kidney transplant during the 12-month study period. This means up to 10 out of 60 participants (16.7%) likely did not complete the final 52-week SGA assessment, falling short of the "nearly all" (>95%) threshold for continuous outcomes.

2. 3.2 If No/probably no to 3.1: Is there evidence that the result was not biased by missing outcome data?
   * Response: No
   * Reason: The authors report using repeated measures ANOVA to analyze longitudinal changes in the SGA score, but there is no mention of multiple imputation, sensitivity analyses, or specific statistical corrections to address the missing data resulting from deaths or study withdrawals.

3. 3.3 If No/probably no to 3.2: Could missingness in the outcome depend on its true value?
   * Response: Probably yes
   * Reason: The missing outcome data were primarily due to death and severe clinical deterioration (transfer to hemodialysis). Because nutritional status (SGA score) is closely tied to overall health, morbidity, and mortality in dialysis patients, it is highly plausible that missingness is related to worsening true SGA values.

4. 3.4 If Yes/probably yes to 3.3: Is it likely that missingness in the outcome depended on its true value?
   * Response: Probably yes
   * Reason: Malnutrition is a known strong predictor of mortality and morbidity in this patient population (as stated by the authors in the introduction). Patients who died or suffered technique failure likely had systematically worse SGA scores compared to the survivors who completed the trial. Because the missingness is directly linked to severe health outcomes, the missing data are very likely not missing at random and depend on their true values.
