## 1.2. Was the allocation sequence concealed until participants were enrolled and assigned to interventions?

While "drawing from a bag" has the potential to be concealed, no details are provided regarding the execution of this procedure to confirm that concealment was maintained (e.g., who prepared the bag, who drew from it, whether the outcome assessor/enroller could see or predict the draw). Given the absence of this information, this must be rated as "No information" rather than assuming adequate concealment.

## 1.3. Did baseline differences between intervention groups suggest a problem with the randomization process?

- **Response: No**
- **Reason:** The independent sample t-test revealed no significant differences between groups on all key baseline variables, including age (p = 0.06), weight (p = 0.391), height (p = 0.406), degree of kyphosis (p = 0.959), and pre-intervention balance score (p = 0.443). Although there was a difference in group sizes (10 vs. 8), the authors attribute this to the unrestricted lot-drawing method used, which is expected to occasionally produce unequal splits by chance in a small sample (n=18) rather than indicating a systematic flaw in the randomization process. No prognostic imbalance is evident that would bias the intervention effect estimate.
