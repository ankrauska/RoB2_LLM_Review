- **No significant OS difference**: 3.5 vs. 3.3 months (HR 0.97, 95% CI 0.77–1.22, P=.96)

- **Open-label design** (Domains 2 and 4) does not by itself elevate risk of bias for an objective, hard endpoint like all-cause mortality — a key nuance of RoB2 that distinguishes it from earlier bias tools. No context-driven deviations were identified, and the ITT analysis was correctly applied.

- **Outcome measurement** (death ascertainment) is objective and not susceptible to assessor bias from unblinding.

Since **all five domains are Low**, the overall RoB2 judgment for overall survival in this trial is **Low risk of bias**, and no directional bias adjustment is warranted (**NA**). This reflects a well-conducted, adequately powered, transparently reported international phase III RCT for this specific outcome.
