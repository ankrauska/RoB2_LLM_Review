- **Trial name**: CTO-IVUS (Chronic Total Occlusion InterVention with drUg-eluting Stents guided by IVUS)

- **Design**: Prospective, multicenter, randomized, 2×2 factorial trial

## PICO Framework
- **Population**: 402 patients with CTO (age 20-80) who had successful guidewire crossing; excluded ACS at presentation, LM disease, in-stent restenosis, LVEF <30%
- **Intervention**: IVUS-guided PCI (n=201)
- **Comparator**: Angiography-guided PCI (n=201)
- **Secondary randomization**: R-ZES (n=201) vs N-BES (n=201)
- **Outcomes**: Primary = cardiac death at 12 months; Secondary = MACE (cardiac death, MI, TVR)

## Key Findings I Noted
1. **Primary endpoint (cardiac death)**: No significant difference (0% IVUS vs 1.0% angiography, P=0.16) — trial did NOT meet primary endpoint
2. **Secondary endpoint (MACE)**: Significantly lower in IVUS group (2.6% vs 7.1%, P=0.035; HR 0.35, 95% CI 0.13-0.97)
3. **Cardiac death/MI composite**: 0% vs 2.0% (P=0.045) — marginal significance
4. **TVR**: No significant difference (2.6% vs 5.2%, P=0.19)
5. **Stent comparison (R-ZES vs N-BES)**: No significant differences in MACE (4.0% vs 5.7%, P=0.45)

## Methodological Points Relevant to Risk-of-Bias Assessment
- Randomization via interactive web-based system, stratified by center
- Single masking only (investigators) — **no blinding of outcome assessors mentioned in registry, though article states clinical events committee was blinded**
- Sample size calculated based on assumed effect sizes extrapolated from a different population (diffuse long lesions, TULIP study) — assumptions described as "arbitrary" by authors themselves
- IVUS optimization criteria "strongly recommended" but not mandated/protocolized — potential performance bias
- No standardized IVUS pullback protocol, timing, or frequency
- Intention-to-treat analysis used
- Discrepancy: registered enrollment target "estimated 400"; actual enrolled 402

## Notable Limitations (author-acknowledged)
- Low event rates overall, particularly for cardiac death/MI — underpowered for primary endpoint
- No serial/systematic IVUS data collection to explain mechanism of benefit
- QCA-only assessment of acute procedural outcomes (no core lab IVUS analysis mentioned for angiography-guided arm, naturally, but limited standardization)
- Minimal stent area achieved was smaller than in MUSIC study benchmarks
- Short follow-up (12 months only) — no data on late stent thrombosis, restenosis patterns, or long-term mortality
- DES comparison (R-ZES vs N-BES) explicitly stated as exploratory/underpowered

## Discrepancy Flag Between Article and Registry
- The registry lists **masking as "SINGLE (Investigator)"**, while the article states the *clinical event committee* was blinded to randomization — these refer to different aspects of blinding (assessment vs. procedural) and should be distinguished carefully in a risk-of-bias table (performance bias vs. detection bias).
