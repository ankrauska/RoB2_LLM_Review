## Overall Assessment

| Item | Judgment |
|------|----------|
| **Overall Risk-of-Bias Judgement** | **High risk** |
| **Predicted Direction of Bias** | **Unpredictable** |

**Overall Justification:** Three of five domains (Missing Outcome Data, Measurement of the Outcome, Selection of the Reported Result) were independently judged **High risk**. Per RoB2 algorithm, a single high-risk domain is sufficient to classify the overall outcome-level judgment as **High risk of bias**; here, three domains converge on this classification, substantially reducing confidence in the reported effect estimates for recurrent thrombotic events.

**Direction Justification:** The direction is **unpredictable** because identified biases operate in opposing directions: differential loss to follow-up (Domain 3) plausibly *underestimates* events in Group A2 (discontinued LMWH), biasing **toward the null**, whereas differential clinical suspicion/testing due to unblinding (Domain 4) plausibly *overestimates* events in Group A2 relative to A1, biasing **away from the null**. Combined with unexplained numerical inconsistencies (Domain 5) whose origin cannot be determined, no single consistent direction of bias can be confidently predicted for this outcome.
