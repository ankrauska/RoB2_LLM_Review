### Final Justification for Overall Judgement

Per the RoB 2 algorithm, **any single domain rated "High risk" results in an overall "High risk" judgement**, regardless of other domains. Domain 4 was escalated to "High" upon critical re-evaluation because:
1. The primary outcome (VAS) is entirely patient-reported and subjective.
2. Blinding of participants was not feasible/achieved, as iontophoresis (electrical current) is sensorially distinguishable from ultrasound-based modalities.
3. No placebo or sham intervention was used in any arm to mask this distinction.
4. This combination — unblinded trial design + fully subjective primary outcome — is explicitly flagged in RoB 2 guidance as a scenario where influence on outcome assessment is *likely*, not merely *possible*.

This finding is further compounded by "Some concerns" in Domain 1 (unconfirmed allocation concealment) and Domain 2 (unblinded participants/personnel, unknown protocol deviations), which independently would not elevate the trial beyond "Some concerns," but reinforce the overall pattern of vulnerability to bias centered on the lack of blinding throughout the trial.

**Direction of bias remains unpredictable** because the trial has three active, non-placebo comparator arms, and the actual observed result (ultrasound outperforming iontophoresis at post-treatment) runs counter to the intuitive expectation-bias hypothesis (that drug-augmented treatments would be perceived/reported as superior).
