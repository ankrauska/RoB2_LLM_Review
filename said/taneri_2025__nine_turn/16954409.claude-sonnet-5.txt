**Study Design:**

- Single center, single cycle per patient
- Conducted May 2000–May 2004

**Population:**
- Unexplained infertility only

- Excluded: prior ART, prior COS-IUI, pelvic surgery history

**Intervention/Comparator:**
- Gonal-F (Follitropin α) vs Metrodin-HP (uFSH) vs Pergonal (hMG)
- Starting dose 75 IU (BMI<25) or 150 IU (BMI≥25), step-up per monitoring
- hCG trigger at follicle ≥16mm; cycle cancelled if >3 follicles ≥16mm or falling E2
- Single IUI 36h post-hCG

**Primary Outcome:** Clinical pregnancy rate (TV-US confirmed at 6 weeks)

**Key Results:**
- Clinical pregnancy rate: rFSH 25.9% vs uFSH 13.8% vs hMG 12.5% (P=0.04/0.046)
- Total gonadotrophin dose significantly lower with rFSH (825 IU vs 1107 vs 1197 IU, P=0.001)
- Dominant follicles ≥16mm significantly higher with rFSH (2.1 vs 1.3 vs 1.4, P=0.001)
- E2 on hCG day higher with rFSH (644 vs 395 vs 455 pg/mL, P=0.001)
- No significant differences: duration of stimulation, multiple pregnancy, miscarriage, OHSS (0% in all groups)

**Methodological Notes for Critical Appraisal:**
- Power calculation based on assumed 10% baseline PR (P1=0.25 assumed for effect size) with 71.63% power (not the conventional 80%) — this is a **notable limitation**, likely underpowered
- Randomization method described (sealed envelopes by nurse) — allocation concealment reasonable but not blinded (open-label, no blinding of clinicians/patients possible given different drug formulations)
- Single insemination/cycle only — limits generalizability to multi-cycle protocols typically used clinically
- No sample size recalculation or interim analysis mentioned
- CONSORT diagram referenced but flow numbers for lost-to-follow-up/exclusions not detailed in text extract
- Statistical tests: chi-square (Yates corrected), t-test, ANOVA, Fisher's exact — appropriate but multiple comparisons across 3 groups without apparent correction mentioned
- P=0.04-0.046 for primary outcome — borderline significance, authors themselves acknowledge this as a limitation ("P value...close to significance")
- No mention of ITT vs per-protocol analysis explicitly, or handling of cancelled cycles in denominator
- Baseline characteristics (Table I) well-balanced across groups (age, BMI, infertility duration)
- Semen parameters stated as comparable but not shown in extracted tables

**Contextual/Conflicting Evidence (from Discussion):**
- Contrasts with Gerli et al. 2004 (no difference in clinical outcomes, only ampoule number)
