Domain 1 was rated **High risk** due to a substantial, outcome-relevant imbalance in the proportion of high-intensity workers (HIW) between the allopurinol (61.5%) and placebo (20%) arms — a subgroup independently shown in this same study (Period A) to have markedly elevated CK/AST levels. This imbalance, combined with unknown allocation concealment, is sufficient to drive the **overall risk-of-bias judgement to High**, irrespective of the more favorable ratings in Domains 3 and 4. This structural imbalance also underlies the predicted **"Favours experimental"** direction of bias, as it likely inflated the apparent CK-lowering effect attributed to allopurinol.
