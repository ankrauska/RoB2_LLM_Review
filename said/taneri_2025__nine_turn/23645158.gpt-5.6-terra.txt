### Overall predicted direction of bias

- **Response:** **Unpredictable**

No prospectively finalized protocol or statistical analysis plan is available in the article to fully exclude selective reporting. These concerns do not establish a direction that necessarily favours magnesium sulfate, placebo, the null, or an effect away from the null.

### Overall Risk-of-bias judgement

- **Judgement:** **Some concerns**  
- **Reason:** Random sequence generation was adequately described, baseline characteristics did not suggest a failure of randomization, outcome data appear complete, and NT Pro-BNP measurement was objective and consistently performed. However, allocation concealment was not reported, which raises concern about the randomization process. In addition, the report does not provide a prospectively finalized analysis plan; the trial registration number appears to reflect registration after the reported 2010–2011 study period. Therefore, low risk of bias cannot be concluded, but the available information does not demonstrate a high risk of bias.
