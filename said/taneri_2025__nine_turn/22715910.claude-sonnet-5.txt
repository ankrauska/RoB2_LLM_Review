**Justification**: Per the RoB 2 algorithm, an overall "High risk of bias" judgement is warranted whenever at least one domain is rated High risk. Domain 5 (Selection of the Reported Result) was judged **High risk** due to the mismatch between the retrospectively registered primary outcome and the outcome analyzed in this article, combined with selective reporting of only one interval out of multiple measured time points and extensive unadjusted subgroup analyses emphasized in the results. This single High-risk domain is sufficient to drive the overall judgement to **High risk**, further compounded by "Some concerns" ratings in three of the remaining four domains (Randomization, Missing Data, Outcome Measurement), which collectively further reduce confidence in the result for the Inhaler Use Technique outcome.
