Based on the provided article and clinical trial protocol, here is a detailed analysis matching the systematic review requirements.

### **1. Study Overview**
- **Trial Identifier:** NCT00450645
- **Study Title:** Residual Vein Thrombosis and the Optimal Duration of Low Molecular Weight Heparin in Cancer Patients With Deep Vein Thrombosis: The Cancer-DACUS Study
- **Sponsor/Lead Institution:** Azienda Ospedaliera Universitaria Policlinico (from protocol)
- **Study Design:** Multicenter, prospective, randomized, open-label trial (allocation: randomized; intervention model: parallel; masking: none).

### **2. Participants and Eligibility**
- **Population:** Adult patients (≥ 18 years) with active cancer (metastatic or locally advanced lung, GI, pancreatic, breast, ovarian, head/neck, or blood cancer) who had a first episode of symptomatic proximal DVT (detected by C-US) and were treated with low molecular weight heparin (LMWH) for 6 months.
- **Key Exclusion Criteria:** ECOG performance status > 2; previous VTE; thrombophilic states; high bleeding risk; active bleeding or recent major bleeding; active gastric/duodenal ulcer; cerebral metastases; severe renal (CrCl < 30 mL/min) or liver insufficiency.
- **Enrollment:** 347 patients were enrolled (out of 409 evaluated).

### **3. Interventions and Grouping**
Patients underwent compression ultrasonography (C-US) at 6 months to assess for Residual Vein Thrombosis (RVT).
- **Group A1 (RVT present, Continued LMWH):** 119 patients randomly assigned to continue LMWH (nadroparin at 75% full dose) for an additional 6 months.
- **Group A2 (RVT present, Stopped LMWH):** 123 patients randomly assigned to discontinue LMWH.
- **Group B (RVT absent):** 105 patients who stopped LMWH (observational/non-randomized arm).

### **4. Outcomes**
- **Primary Endpoint:** Time to recurrent VTE (DVT and/or PE) during 1 year after completion/discontinuation of LMWH.
- **Secondary Endpoints:** Major bleeding, minor bleeding, and death due to cancer progression.

### **5. Key Results**
- **Recurrent VTE (1-year follow-up after LMWH):** 
  - Group A1 (RVT + Continued): 18/119 (plus 4 events during the initial 6 months post-randomization, total 22/119 or 18.5%).
  - Group A2 (RVT + Stopped): 27/123 (21.9%).
  - Group B (No RVT + Stopped): 3/105 (2.8%).
- **Statistical Comparisons for Efficacy:** 
  - Adjusted HR for Group A2 vs A1 = 1.37 (95% CI, 0.7 to 2.5; P = .311). Continuing LMWH in RVT-positive patients did not significantly reduce recurrence.
  - Adjusted HR for Group A1 vs Group B = 6.0 (95% CI, 1.7 to 21.2; P = .005).
- **Safety (Bleeding):** 
  - Major bleeding: 7 events (5.8%) in Group A1 vs. 2 (1.6%) in A2 vs. 2 (1.9%) in B (P = .109 overall; HR for A1 vs A2 = 3.78, P = .102). 
- **Mortality:** 42 deaths (12.1%) overall, all due to cancer progression. No significant difference among groups.

### **6. Conclusions**
The absence of RVT at 6 months identifies a subset of cancer patients with a very low risk of recurrent VTE (2.8%), for whom stopping anticoagulation is safe. In patients with RVT, extending LMWH treatment for up to 1 year did not result in a statistically significant reduction in VTE recurrence compared to stopping at 6 months.
