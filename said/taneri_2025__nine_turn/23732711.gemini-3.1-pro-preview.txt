* Response: Probably yes

The authors explicitly note, "There were no differences in phenotype, sun exposure, or pretrial sunscreen use between the intervention and control groups at baseline," and the slight numerical difference in β-carotene vs. placebo assignment (P = 0.06) is compatible with chance.
