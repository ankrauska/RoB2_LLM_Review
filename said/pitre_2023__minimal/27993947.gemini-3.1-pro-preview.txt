**1. bias due to randomization: low risk of bias**
a. Judgement: low risk of bias
b. Rationale: The trial used a computer-generated random sequence with stratification. The allocation assignment was done after the first study visit using computer software, indicating adequate sequence generation and likely adequate concealment. Baseline characteristics (Table 1) were well balanced between the intervention groups, suggesting no problems with the randomization process. "Randomization was performed by means of a computer-generated random sequence with stratification for age, gender, and positive family history for CHD..."

**2. deviations from intended intervention: low risk of bias**
a. Judgement: low risk of bias
b. Rationale: Participants and personnel were not blinded to the intervention (as the nature of the intervention was the disclosure of genetic risk). However, there is no evidence that there were deviations from the intended intervention that arose because of the trial context that would have affected the outcome. Data from almost all randomized participants were analyzed in the groups to which they were assigned.

**3. missing outcome data: low risk of bias**
a. Judgement: low risk of bias
b. Rationale: Outcome data were available for nearly all randomized participants. Out of 207 randomized participants, 206 completed the SDM survey, meaning the missing data rate was extremely low and could not have introduced bias.

**4. measurement of the outcome: some concerns**
a. Judgement: some concerns
b. Rationale: The outcome was measured using a validated survey (SDM-Q), which is a participant-reported outcome. Because participants were aware of the intervention they received (disclosure of genetic risk or not), and the outcome relies on self-reporting of their perception of the shared decision-making process, it is possible that their assessment of the outcome could have been influenced by their knowledge of the intervention received. 

**5. selective reporting: low risk of bias**
a. Judgement: low risk of bias
b. Rationale: "Shared decision making (as assessed by survey)" was pre-specified as a secondary outcome in the trial registry (NCT01936675). The reported results match the pre-specified outcome, and there is no indication that the reported result was selected from multiple outcome measurements or multiple analyses.
