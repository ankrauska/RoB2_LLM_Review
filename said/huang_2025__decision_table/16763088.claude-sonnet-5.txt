**1.1**: Y (95% - High Certainty)
Reason: The article explicitly states "Randomization for allocation of either of the two etch and primer systems to the upper right quadrant was undertaken using a block randomization method as described by Roberts and Torgerson (1998)." This is an explicitly described valid method of sequence generation.

**1.2**: NI (90% - Moderate/High Certainty)
Reason: The article states "The operators were unaware of the assignment until the patient was entered into the trial" but does not describe the specific concealment mechanism (e.g., sequentially numbered opaque containers, central allocation, opaque sealed envelopes). **1.3**: N (90% - Moderate/High Certainty)
