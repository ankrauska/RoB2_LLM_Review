Domain 2
2.1 : Y (100%)
The study is described as an "open study design" in the discussion section, and the clinical trial registration also specifies "Masking: NONE", meaning participants were aware of their assigned intervention.
2.2 : Y (100%)
Due to the open-label design of the trial, the carers and people delivering the interventions were aware of the participants' assigned interventions.
2.3 : NI (100%)
The article does not provide information regarding whether there were any significant non-protocol interventions or whether they were balanced across intervention groups.
2.4 : N (95%)
Out of 233 randomized patients, 227 took at least one dose of the study medication, meaning that failure to implement the intervention occurred in less than 10% of the participants.
2.5 : N (95%)
The article reports that median compliance was very high (98.6% and 97.8% in the two groups), indicating that non-adherence affected less than 10% of the participants.
2.6 : N (100%)
The study utilized "intent-to-treat (ITT) analysis" and "per-protocol (PP) analysis", which are considered inappropriate analytical methods to evaluate the effectiveness of persistent interventions (i.e., the effect of adhering to intervention).
Result of the domain (100%)
High risk
Because the answer to questions 2.1 and 2.2 is "Y", the answer to 2.3 is "NI", and the answer to 2.6 is "N", the assessment result for Domain 2 is "High risk".
