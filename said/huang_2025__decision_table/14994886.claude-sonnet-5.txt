## Domain 1: Risk of bias arising from the randomization process

**1.1**: Result: **NI** (Confidence: 85%)
Reason: The article states "Insertion of either a 0.014 or 0.016 inch wire was by random selection" and "patients were randomly selected." However, no specific method of sequence generation (e.g., computer-generated numbers, random number tables, coin toss, block randomization) is described anywhere in the Methods section.

**1.2**: Result: **NI** (Confidence: 90%)
Reason: There is no mention of allocation concealment methods such as central allocation, sequentially numbered opaque containers, or sealed envelopes anywhere in the article.

**1.3**: Result: **NI** (Confidence: 80%)
Reason: While group sizes (56 vs 53) and gender distribution within each wire group are reported, there is no explicit statistical comparison of baseline characteristics between the two wire-size groups. The gender comparison mentioned in the Results pertains to the outcome (pain perception), not baseline demographic comparability between groups.

**Result of Domain 1**: **Some concerns** (Confidence: 85%)
Reason: Since 1.2 = NI and 1.3 = NI (falls under "N, NI, or PN" category), the domain defaults to "Some concerns" per the decision rules.
