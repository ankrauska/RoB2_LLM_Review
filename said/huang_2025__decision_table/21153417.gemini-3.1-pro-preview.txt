**Article ID**: Barrios, 2011
**Outcome Name**: Muscle damage
**registration number**: None

Domain 1
1.1 : Y (100%)
The article clearly states that "In period B, crew members were divided into two groups according to random numbers."
1.2 : NI (100%)
The article mentions that sailors were blind regarding group assignment and a placebo was used, but it does not describe the specific method used to conceal the allocation sequence (such as central allocation or opaque envelopes).
1.3 : Y (95%)
There were noticeable baseline differences between the intervention and control groups regarding the physical work intensity of the sailors. The allopurinol group had 8 high-intensity work (HIW) sailors and 1 low-intensity work (LIW) sailor, whereas the placebo group had 2 HIW and 4 LIW sailors. Since HIW sailors had significantly different anthropometric profiles and higher baseline muscle damage markers, this imbalance suggests a problem with the randomization process.
Result of the domain: High risk (100%)
The allocation sequence was generated adequately, but allocation concealment was not mentioned (NI), and there were significant baseline imbalances between the groups that suggest a problem with the randomization process (Y).

Domain 2
2.1 : N (100%)
The article explicitly states that "Sailors were blind regarding the group assignment" and that the control group received a placebo.
2.2 : N (100%)
The article states that the control group received a placebo intervention as part of the blinding procedure.
2.3 : NI (100%)
The article does not provide information regarding whether there were any significant non-protocol interventions balanced across the groups.
2.4 : N (90%)
Implementation of the intervention was successful for most participants who started the phase. The four sailors who declined did so before the intervention phase started, not as a failure of implementation during the trial.
2.5 : NI (100%)
The article does not provide any information regarding adherence to the assigned intervention regimen.
2.6 : NI (100%)
The article does not provide information on the specific analytical methods (such as ITT or per-protocol) used to estimate the effect of adhering to the intervention.
Result of the domain: Low risk (85%)
Participants and personnel were effectively blinded through the use of a placebo, and there were no indications of significant deviations from the intended interventions that could have affected the outcomes.

3.2 : Y (90%)
Since data were available for all participants in the intervention phase, there is no missing data that could bias the results.
3.3 : N (95%)
There was no missing outcome data for the participants in the intervention phase.
3.4 : N (95%)

Result of the domain: Low risk (95%)
Outcome data were available for all participants randomized into the intervention phase.
