## Domain 2: Risk of bias due to deviations from intended interventions

**2.1**: Y (90%)
Participants were aware whether they received free strips (NC) or had to purchase strips (C); this key difference in resource provision could not be blinded to participants.

**2.2**: Y (85%)
The research nurse who distributed strips and supplies would necessarily know which group each patient belonged to (free vs. limited supply of strips).

**2.3**: NI (70%)

**2.4**: Y (95%)

**2.5**: Y (85%)

## Domain 3: Risk of bias due to missing outcome data

**3.2**: N (90%)

**3.3**: Y (80%)
