## Domain 1: Risk of bias arising from the randomization process

**1.1**: NI (85%)
The article states pharmacists were "randomized to" postal mail, email, or hybrid groups, but no specific method of sequence generation (e.g., computer-generated random numbers, random number tables, block randomization) is described anywhere in the Methods section.

**1.2**: NI (90%)
There is no mention of allocation concealment, central allocation, sequentially numbered containers, or opaque envelopes anywhere in the article.

**1.3**: Y (80%)
Table 1 shows substantial baseline demographic differences across delivery-method groups (e.g., Non-Hispanic White: 86.3% postal vs. 68.7% email vs. 74.0% hybrid; Black: 3.2% postal vs. 26.4% email vs. 19.2% hybrid; practice site and location differences). Notably, the samples for postal/hybrid groups were drawn from a different commercial database (Medical Marketing Service) than the email group (Statlistics), indicating the "randomization" was not from a single homogeneous sampling frame. This raises real concern that the process generating group assignment did not yield comparable groups.

**Result of Domain 1**: High risk (85%)
Because 1.2 = NI and 1.3 = Y, per the algorithm this yields "High risk."
