Domain 1
1.1 : NI (100%)
The article states that participants were "assigned by simple random selection" and mentions "sex-stratified randomization," but it does not specify the exact method used to generate the random sequence (e.g., computer-generated random numbers).
1.2 : Y (100%)
The article clearly states that "the allocation sequence was blinded through the use of sealed envelopes."
1.3 : N (100%)
The article reports that there were no significant baseline differences between the two groups regarding age and pre-test dependent variables, and Table 1 confirms the homogeneity.

Low risk
Reason you summarized
The allocation sequence was properly concealed (1.2 is Y), and there were no significant baseline imbalances (1.3 is N), leading to a low risk of bias despite the method of sequence generation not being fully detailed (1.1 is NI).
