Domain 2
2.1 : Y (100%)
The article clearly states that "Blinding to treatment was not undertaken", meaning participants were aware of their assigned intervention.
2.2 : Y (100%)
Since blinding was not undertaken, the carers and people delivering the interventions were aware of the participants' assigned intervention.
2.3 : NI (100%)
The article does not provide information regarding important non-protocol interventions and whether they were balanced across groups.
2.4 : Y (100%)

Domain 3
3.1 : N (95%)
The outcome is a binary variable (cured vs. not cured). At 2 years, 100 out of 134 in PB-MDT and 103 out of 134 in ROM-6 were followed up, meaning about 25% were missing. Although there were many observed events (cured), the number of non-events was very small compared to the number of missing outcomes, leading to a substantial proportion of missing data.
3.2 : N (100%)
There is no evidence that the analysis used methods like inverse probability weighting to correct for bias due to missing data.

3.4 : Y (90%)
