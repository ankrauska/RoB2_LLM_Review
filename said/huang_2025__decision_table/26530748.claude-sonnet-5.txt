## Domain 1: Risk of bias arising from the randomization process

**1.2**: NI (85%)

## Domain 3: Risk of bias due to missing outcome data

**3.1**: N (90%)
Reason: Of 427 randomized patients, only 202 evaluable 7-year questionnaires (and 80 10-year) were obtained. This represents well under 90% of the randomized population with complete continuous outcome data.

**3.2**: N (90%)
Reason: No inverse probability weighting, sensitivity analysis, or other bias-correction method was used to address missing outcome data.

**3.3**: PY (70%)
Reason: Eligibility for follow-up required patients to be "alive and disease-free"; the discussion further notes that older, more comorbid patients were more likely to have died, and QoL/global health is plausibly related to health status, though not explicitly confirmed as directly tied to the global health score itself.

**3.4**: Y (75%)
Reason: The discussion explicitly states that non-responders were more likely to be older with more comorbidities, and that mortality was more likely in this subgroup — suggesting missingness is related to the true value of the global health outcome measure.

**Result of Domain 3**: High risk (85%)
Reason: 3.1 = N, 3.2 = N, 3.3 = PY, 3.4 = Y → per algorithm, yields "High risk."
