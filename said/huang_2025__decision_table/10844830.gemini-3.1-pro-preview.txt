Domain 3
3.1 : Y (95-100%)
The study allocated 20 patients (10 per group). The results and figures report data for all 10 patients in group 1. In group 2, an outlier was excluded from statistical analysis for ET-1 ("Group 2 had an outlier... These data were excluded from the statistical analysis"), leaving 9/10 (90%) for the ET-1 outcome. But for venous ET-1 specifically, the text says "arterial and mixed venous ET-1 concentrations...". The outlier was high *arterial* ET-1 values, but still, at least 90% had complete data. For a continuous variable, 95% or more is Y. But wait, 19/20 = 95%. So 95% of the total randomized participants had complete outcome data.
3.2 : N (75-94%)
There is no evidence that the result was not biased by missing outcome data, and no specific bias correction methods like inverse probability weighting were used for the missing data (the outlier).
3.3 : N (75-94%)
The missing data was due to the exclusion of an outlier ("Group 2 had an outlier with high arterial ET-1 values... These data were excluded from the statistical analysis in order to simplify statistical interpretation"). This exclusion does not imply that missingness depended on the true value of the venous ET-1 concentration or health issues related to dropout.
3.4 : N (75-94%)
The article explains the exclusion of the outlier was to "simplify statistical interpretation" and found no explanation for the finding based on patient data, suggesting the missingness (exclusion) was not dependent on the true outcome value in a way that introduces bias.
Result of the domain : Low risk (95-100%)
Since 3.1 is Y, the assessment result for Domain 3 is "Low risk".
