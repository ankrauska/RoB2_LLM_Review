2.4 : N (95%)
Withdrawal rates were low: "7.9% for patients treated with escitalopram (13 patients), and 8.9% for patients treated with placebo (14 patients)." This is less than 10%.

The analysis was based on Intention-to-Treat (ITT): "All efficacy analyses in the double-blind treatment period were conducted using the intention-to-treat (ITT) population".
Result of the domain: Low risk (95%)
Based on the blinding and low withdrawal rates, with ITT analysis, the risk of bias due to deviations from the intended interventions is low.
