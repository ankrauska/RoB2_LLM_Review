Domain 2
2.1 : Y (100%)
The trial involved a discretionary sunscreen group without a placebo sunscreen because it was considered unethical, so participants were aware of their sunscreen assignment.

The participants self-applied the sunscreen, meaning the people delivering the intervention (the participants themselves) were aware of their assigned intervention.
2.3 : Y (95%)

2.4 : Y (100%)
