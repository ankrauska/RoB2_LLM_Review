## Domain 1: Risk of bias arising from the randomization process

**1.1: NI (High Certainty, 98%)**

**1.2: NI (High Certainty, 98%)**  
No allocation-concealment procedure, such as central allocation, sequentially numbered opaque envelopes, or sequentially numbered identical vaccine containers, was reported. The open-label design does not itself provide information on allocation concealment.

**1.3: N (High Certainty, 96%)**

## Domain 3: Risk of bias due to missing outcome data

**3.1: Y (High Certainty, 98%)**  
Anti-HAV GMT data at 1 month were available for 121 of 127 randomized participants: 59/64 in the Vaqta group and 62/63 in the Avaxim group. Overall, 121/127 = 95.3% had complete observed outcome data, which meets the ≥95% threshold for a continuous outcome.

**3.2: N (High Certainty, 94%)**  
No inverse-probability weighting, sensitivity analysis, or other appropriate method was reported to demonstrate that missing outcome data did not bias the GMT result. Intention-to-treat analysis alone does not establish correction for missing-data bias.

**3.3: NI (Moderate Certainty, 72%)**  
Four participants lacked both pre- and post-vaccination serological results, but the article does not report why these serological data were unavailable. Therefore, it cannot be determined whether missingness depended on the true anti-HAV antibody titre.

**3.4: NI (Moderate Certainty, 70%)**  
The article does not provide sufficient information on reasons for missing serological outcomes or characteristics of participants with missing data to determine whether missingness was likely related to true antibody titres.

**Result of Domain 3: Low risk (High Certainty, 98%)**  
Complete 1-month GMT outcome data were available for 95.3% of randomized participants, meeting the predefined threshold for low risk from missing outcome data.

## Domain 4: Risk of bias in measurement of the outcome

**4.1: N (High Certainty, 97%)**  
Anti-HAV antibody concentrations were measured using a modified quantitative HAVAB assay, calibrated using a WHO standard serum immune globulin. The assay quantified antibody concentrations in mIU/mL and measured levels down to 6 mIU/mL. This is an appropriate and reliable method for measuring anti-HAV antibody GMT.

**4.2: N (High Certainty, 96%)**  
Both groups had anti-HAV antibody titres measured before vaccination and at the same scheduled follow-up time point, 21–35 days after vaccination. The same serological method and measurement threshold were used for both groups.

**4.3: NI (Moderate Certainty, 78%)**  
Serological evaluations were performed by Merck Research Laboratories, but the article does not state whether laboratory outcome assessors were blinded to vaccine assignment.

**4.4: N (High Certainty, 98%)**  
Anti-HAV antibody titre is an objective laboratory-based outcome. Assessment is not expected to be influenced by knowledge of the intervention received.

**4.5: N (High Certainty, 96%)**  
There is no clear evidence that knowledge of vaccine assignment was likely to influence the objective laboratory measurement of anti-HAV antibody concentrations.

**Result of Domain 4: Low risk (High Certainty, 96%)**  
The outcome was measured objectively using the same quantitative serological assay at comparable time points in both groups. Although assessor blinding was not reported, knowledge of vaccine assignment is unlikely to influence an objective laboratory antibody result.

## Domain 5: Risk of bias in selection of the reported result

**5.1: NI (High Certainty, 97%)**  
No trial registration number, protocol, or pre-specified analysis plan finalized before unblinded outcome data became available was reported. Therefore, it cannot be confirmed that the reported GMT result was analyzed according to a pre-specified analysis plan.

**5.2: N (High Certainty, 93%)**  
The primary evaluation criterion was explicitly stated as comparison of anti-HAV antibody GMT between vaccines 1 month post-vaccination. The reported result corresponds to this specified outcome measurement and time point. There is no evidence that the 1-month GMT result was selected from multiple eligible measurements on the basis of results.

**5.3: NI (Moderate Certainty, 78%)**  
The study reported both intention-to-treat and per-protocol analyses but presented only per-protocol immunogenicity results. Although the authors stated that the analyses were close, the absence of a pre-specified analysis plan means it cannot be determined whether the reported numerical result was selected from multiple eligible analyses based on the results.

**Result of Domain 5: Some concerns (High Certainty, 91%)**  
The result corresponds to the stated primary 1-month GMT outcome, but no pre-specified analysis plan or trial registration was reported, and only per-protocol immunogenicity results were presented despite both intention-to-treat and per-protocol analyses being conducted.
