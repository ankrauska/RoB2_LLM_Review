## Domain 1: Risk of bias arising from the randomization process

**1.1**: Y (95%)
*Reason*: The article explicitly states "a sequence of random integers generated by a true random number generator (Haahr, 2004)" was used to determine allocation order — a clearly described adequate random sequence generation method.

**1.2**: NI (85%)
*Reason*: The article states booklets were placed "in a single pile in an order determined by" the random sequence, then distributed in a lecture theatre/lab. No mention of central allocation, sequentially numbered opaque containers, or other concealment safeguards is provided.

**1.3**: N (95%)
*Reason*: MANOVA on pre-intervention behaviour (F(4,141)=1.37, ns) and cognitions (F(6,139)=0.06, ns) revealed no significant baseline differences between intervention and control groups; differences attributable to chance.

**Result of Domain 1**: Some concerns (85%)
*Reason*: 1.2 = NI and 1.3 = N → per the algorithm this yields "Some concerns."

## Domain 4: Risk of bias in measurement of the outcome

**4.1**: N (90%)
*Reason*: Intention was measured using a validated 5-item scale with high internal consistency (Cronbach's α = .90 pre, .93 post), an appropriate and reliable measurement method.

**4.2**: N (85%)
*Reason*: Both groups completed identical measures at the same time points using the same instrument/thresholds.

**4.3**: Y (85%)
*Reason*: Intention is a patient-reported outcome, and participants were aware of which task (intervention persuasive messages/implementation intention exercise vs. control questions about the questionnaire) they had completed, making them aware of their own group assignment.

**4.4**: Y (90%)
*Reason*: Intention is a subjective, patient-reported measure, inherently susceptible to influence by knowledge of the intervention received.

**4.5**: Y (80%)
*Reason*: Given the salient difference in content between the persuasive intervention task and the neutral control task, participants' self-reported intentions immediately post-task were plausibly influenced by their awareness of which task they completed (demand characteristics).

**Result of Domain 4**: High risk (85%)
*Reason*: 4.1=N, 4.2=N, 4.3=Y, 4.4=Y, 4.5=Y → per algorithm this combination yields "High risk."
