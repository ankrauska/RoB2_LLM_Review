**1. bias due to randomization: high risk of bias**
* **a. Judgement:** High risk of bias
* **b. Rationale:** The trial report states that "Random assignment was determined by a sealed-envelope technique based on clinical chart number with a block size of 10." Allocation based on clinical chart numbers is a systematic, non-random method of sequence generation (often termed quasi-random). Because the sequence generation was not truly random, and this could allow predictability of assignments (especially with a fixed block size of 10 and no blinding), there is a high risk of bias arising from the randomization process.

**2. deviations from intended intervention: some concerns**
* **a. Judgement:** Some concerns
* **b. Rationale:** The trial was open-label, explicitly stating that "Patients and treating physicians were not masked to treatment or status of RVT." Because participants and personnel were aware of the assigned interventions (continuing vs. stopping LMWH), there is potential for deviations from the intended intervention that arose because of the trial context. While the study states that "Data were analyzed on an intention-to-treat basis," there is insufficient information to entirely rule out the occurrence and impact of trial-context deviations (such as the use of other non-protocol anticoagulants or changes in behavior), leading to some concerns. 

**3. missing outcome data: low risk of bias**
* **a. Judgement:** Low risk of bias
* **b. Rationale:** Outcome data appear to be available for all randomized participants. The CONSORT diagram indicates that all patients allocated to the intervention groups were included in the analysis (119 in group A1 and 123 in group A2). While 42 patients died during the 12-month follow-up, these deaths were documented as resulting from cancer progression rather than loss to follow-up, and all fatal events were evaluated. Therefore, there is no evidence that the results were biased by missing outcome data.

**4. measurement of the outcome: low risk of bias**
* **a. Judgement:** Low risk of bias
* **b. Rationale:** Although the patients and treating physicians were not blinded, the trial report specifies that "All outcomes and deaths were evaluated by a central committee whose members were unaware of the patient’s name, enrolling center, results of RVT assays, and group assignment." Since the actual outcome assessors (the central adjudication committee) were blinded to the intervention received, the assessment of the outcome is unlikely to have been influenced by knowledge of the intervention. 

**5. selective reporting: low risk of bias**
* **a. Judgement:** Low risk of bias
* **b. Rationale:** The primary outcome reported in the publication is "recurrent venous thromboembolism (VTE)", which perfectly matches the primary outcome pre-specified in the trial registration (NCT00450645): "Recurrent Deep Vein Thrombosis and/or Pulmonary Embolism". The results are reported according to the pre-specified plan, and there is no indication that the reported result was selected from multiple measurements or analyses based on the direction or significance of the findings.
