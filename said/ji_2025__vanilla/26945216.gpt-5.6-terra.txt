- TENS and IFT involved visibly different electrode configurations and electrical-current treatments, whereas the control group received standard care alone.

Analysis:
Although the authors state that participants were blinded to allocation, there was no placebo/sham intervention for the control group. Participants receiving TENS or IFT underwent an additional 20-minute electrical stimulation procedure with electrodes, while control participants did not. Thus, control participants would almost certainly know they were not receiving the electrical treatment. Therefore, participant blinding was not credible across the intervention groups.
