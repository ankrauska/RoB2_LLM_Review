Furthermore, the outcome data retrieved for a subset of the defaulters showed that some still had active disease. Because a substantial proportion of participants were excluded from the final analysis and their outcomes were not uniformly positive, there is clear potential that excluding them could have had a substantial impact on the final results (violating the intention-to-treat principle).
