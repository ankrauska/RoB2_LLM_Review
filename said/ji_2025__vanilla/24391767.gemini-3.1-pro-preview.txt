Analysis:
The study evaluated the outcome of image quality. Patients who could not tolerate their randomly assigned MR scanner due to claustrophobia crossed over to the other scanner before imaging began. Since it is impossible to evaluate the image quality of a scanner that was not used, the investigators analyzed the images based on the scanner that actually performed the imaging (an "as-treated" approach). The results describe evaluating the 48 short-bore and 55 open MR examinations (grouping by received intervention rather than the originally assigned 44 vs. 49 randomized patients shown in baseline Table 1). According to the RoB 2 guidelines, "as-treated" analyses or naïve "per-protocol" analyses are considered inappropriate for estimating the effect of assignment to the intervention.
